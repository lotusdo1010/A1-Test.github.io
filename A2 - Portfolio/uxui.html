SHOWCASE
The work is an interactive poem website adapted from Léopold Sédar Senghor's "Dear White Brother" aimed at countering racial prejudice. Thru the scrolling action, users witness the contrasting shades between black and white people thru various events. The minimalist interface (UI) combined with a smooth scrolling experience (UX) guides the viewer's emotions thru each poetic rhythm. The combination of digital design and literature creates a visually sophisticated experience rich in humanistic value.

SHOWCASE
The work is an interactive web application "Seed to Soul," detailing the 7 steps of growing and caring for roses from seed to harvest. The interface (UI) uses a warm flat illustration style, combined with an intuitive toolbar that makes it easy for users to interact. The user experience (UX) is optimized thanks to a step-by-step guidance system and instant feedback thru direct drag-and-drop actions on the screen. This digital design provides a lively and memorable "learning by doing" experience for learners.

SHOWCASE
The work is the interface of the "Geometric Genesis" exhibition website, featuring a Bauhaus art style with an impressive retro color scheme. The UI design divides information and product sections visually, combining an optimized and smooth UX experience for the entire process of selecting tickets, purchasing souvenirs, and making payments. The website also achieves high compatibility on both computers and mobile devices, providing an incredibly seamless user journey.
