The work is a 58-second self-portrait video documenting the transformation into the character Nahida (Genshin Impact). The video seamlessly combines live-action and motion graphics, impressively utilizing editing techniques such as beat matching, kaleidoscope effects, and hand-drawn 2D frame-by-frame animation that directly interacts with the character, perfectly synchronized with the synth-pop background music and sound effects (SFX).

SHOWCASE
This is a short essay clip analyzing the development of self-driving cars at the present time and in the near future. The clip is presented with scribbled drawings that hit the main points, making it easier for viewers to understand while also enjoying the cheerful images that prevent the clip from being boring. Recording personal voiceovers allows us to inject our genuine emotions to guide the audience's trends.

The work uses seamless transition techniques to turn the pages of a book into doors opening to imaginary worlds. The camera angles shift fluidly from close-ups to wide shots, creating an engaging visual rhythm. The contrast between the black-and-white line-art in real life and the saturated colors in the fantastical worlds has fully celebrated the power of imagination.
